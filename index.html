    Keep These Distinctions Active

    The guide becomes coherent when four neighboring ideas stay separate.

    Non-linear cinematics

    The topology of authored content: branches, non-mandatory scenes, reordered beats, alternate participants and reconvergence.

    Systemic cinematics

    Rules select or adapt staging, cameras, gestures, lighting, timing and performers from game state.

    Emergent narrative

    The story a player interprets from interacting simulation systems; it may have no conventional cinematic.

    Productive overlap

    Authored intent, systemic realization: automate reusable grammar while protecting the dramatic center.

    A–Z

    Fluency Vocabulary

    A compact language for discussing the collection precisely.

    Agency
    Meaningful action whose results the player can perceive.
    Authored glue
    Connective content that preserves continuity between variably ordered scenes.
    Combinatorial explosion
    Production and test cost growing faster than the number of choices.
    Consequence
    A state change that affects later possibilities or outcomes.
    Coverage
    The set of shots needed to present and edit a scene.
    Drama manager
    A system that selects or schedules events to regulate dramatic form, pacing or intensity.
    Emergent narrative
    A story interpreted from interacting simulation systems.
    Fail-forward
    Failure changes the story instead of forcing repetition.
    Foldback
    Branches produce local variation before returning to shared content.
    Micro-reactivity
    Small acknowledgements that make a system feel attentive.
    Narrative debt
    Future content required to make an earlier choice coherent or visible.
    Player-as-actor
    The player retains expressive participation inside cinematic staging.
    Procedural staging
    Rule-based placement, movement and behavior of performers.
    Recognition vs. consequence
    Acknowledgement of an action versus a material change to the possibility space.
    Signal-to-noise
    The relation between essential narrative information and competing stimuli.
    Storylet
    A modular narrative unit selected when its preconditions are met.
